📊 Business Process Mapping Project

A Beginner-Level Business Analysis & Process Improvement Case Study

📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on understanding and mapping a business process using structured Business Analysis techniques.

As a fresher in Business Analysis, my objective was to:

Understand how a business process works

Identify inefficiencies or gaps

Create visual process flows

Suggest improvements based on analysis

This project demonstrates practical exposure to:

Business Process Modeling (BPMN)

Requirement Gathering

Stakeholder Thinking

Analytical & Critical Thinking

Process Documentation

🎯 Problem Statement

Many organizations operate with unclear or undocumented processes. This leads to:

Delays in operations

Communication gaps

Rework and inefficiencies

Poor decision-making

The goal of this project was to:

Map the current business process (As-Is), identify pain points, and design an improved process (To-Be).

🧠 Approach & Methodology
Step 1: Understanding the Business Process

Studied the current workflow

Identified stakeholders involved

Noted process inputs and outputs

Step 2: Requirement Elicitation

Identified business needs

Defined process objectives

Documented functional and non-functional requirements

Step 3: Process Mapping (As-Is)

Created flow diagrams

Defined decision points

Identified bottlenecks

Step 4: Gap Analysis

Compared current process with expected outcomes

Identified inefficiencies

Suggested process improvements

Step 5: To-Be Process Design

Redesigned the workflow

Reduced redundant steps

Improved process clarity
